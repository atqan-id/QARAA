# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

QARAA contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation safe, respectful, and constructive. Treat people with dignity across differences in identity, background, experience, language, and viewpoint. Technical disagreement is welcome when it focuses on evidence and the work.

Acceptable conduct includes giving actionable feedback, acknowledging mistakes, respecting privacy and consent, and taking particular care with religious text and culturally sensitive data.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, reviews, project events, and public representation of QARAA. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude participants whose conduct conflicts with this standard. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and protect reporters from retaliation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner’s private contact channel. Do not disclose sensitive reports in a public issue. If no private channel is visible, use the hosting platform’s abuse-reporting mechanism and ask the repository owner to establish one without including incident details publicly.

Maintainers receiving a report should acknowledge it, limit access to those needed for review, avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate the outcome to the reporter when it is safe and lawful to do so.
